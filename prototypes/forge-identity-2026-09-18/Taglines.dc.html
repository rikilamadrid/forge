Three taglines in the hero

Same lockup, same subline, different first sentence.
01
forge-local-ai-kit

Where local intelligence is shaped into tools.

Says what it does Carries the whole metaphor in plain words. Longest of the three, and the only one that explains Forge to someone who has never heard of it.
02
forge-local-ai-kit

Every tool bears the mark.

Says what it promises The best line of the three and the worst opener: it means nothing until you already know what the mark is. Earns its place further down the page.
03
forge-local-ai-kit

Shaped locally. Marked, measured, yours.

Says what it guarantees Covers local, hallmark, evidence and ownership in six words. The rhythm is strong, but four clauses in a row edges toward a slogan.
